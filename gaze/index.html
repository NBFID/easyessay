Writeout with eye tracking

Paste the essay, calibrate the camera, then write the essay out. The reference stays on screen; every time you look up at it the app records which sentence you needed, and the report shows what you don't know yet.

Sit as you will when writing, about an arm's length from the screen, with light on your face rather than behind you. The calibration only holds for this window position, so the app goes fullscreen; recalibrate if you move the laptop or yourself much. The last calibration is kept and offered again next time. Nothing leaves your computer: the camera is read in the browser and the session is saved in this browser only.
